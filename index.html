GentleReminders

Adults-Only Celebration

To provide an atmosphere where all guests may fully relax and enjoy the festivities, we have chosen to host an adults-only celebration. While we hold your children dear to our hearts, we respectfully request that our wedding and reception be attended only by the invited adult guests. We hope this advance notice allows ample time to make the necessary arrangements. We are sincerely grateful for your understanding and cooperation, and we look forward to sharing this memorable occasion with you.

Dress Code

Our palette for the day is soft sage green, accented with emerald and gray. Please refer to the guide below for specific attire. We kindly ask that white be reserved for the bride.

← Swipe or click to view all dress code references →

Courtesy for Our Creatives

As we celebrate this special occasion, we kindly ask our guests to be mindful of our photographers and videographers. While you are welcome to take your own photos, please refrain from stepping into the aisle or blocking their view, particularly during the processional and recessional. Your cooperation will help us capture these cherished memories perfectly.

Arrival

To ensure everything runs smoothly, please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony starts. This will give you time to find your seat, take in the beautiful setup, and be fully present for our special moment.

Sending Off

As our celebration draws to a close, we warmly invite you to remain with us until the end of the program and join us for our farewell send-off. Your presence on this special day means so much to us, and we would be delighted to share one final moment with you before we begin our new chapter together. Thank you for celebrating with us.

RSVP Guidelines

We kindly request your RSVP by August 31, 2026. Seats will be reserved only for guests who confirm their attendance. RSVPs received from September 1, 2026 onward, will no longer be accommodated. This invitation is exclusively for the person(s) who received this link and is non-transferable. No RSVP, no seat reservation. Thank you for your understanding, and we look forward to celebrating with you.

Gifts

Having you share in our special day is a gift in itself. Should you wish to offer a gift, any gesture of thoughtfulness and generosity will be warmly received and deeply appreciated as we embark on this new journey together. Should you wish to bless us with a monetary gift, our QR code is available on the RSVP page for your convenience.
